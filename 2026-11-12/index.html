Cowboy Church of Brenham, 4400 Hwy 105 · Map
Kid Approved0–45–89–11FundraiserOutdoors

The very first Rodeo Round Up from Here I Am Orphan Ministries: youth rodeo events, a petting zoo, hayrides, cornhole and concessions.

Hey, y'all

Sunny and in the low 70s all weekend!!! (Grab a jacket for the chilly mornings, though.) Saturday is STACKED: 1800s-style horse races with stick horse races for the kids at Washington-on-the-Brazos, the very first Forever Families Rodeo Round Up at Cowboy Church, and a morning of fishing with a ranger at Lake Somerville. Sunday, the lake has animal skulls to touch and a meteor shower to watch. And for the adults: a Texas dance hall night, a Fleetwood Mac tribute, a paint and sip and an art meetup at the biergarten.

Picks for each day

  • ThuTake the littles on the Tiny Trailblazers hike at Lake Somerville. Night out: the Brenham Art Alliance meetup at the biergarten.
  • FriA quiet one, so rest up for Saturday! Try breakfast at The Hard Scramble, one of Brenham's two brand new breakfast spots (open Thursday to Sunday, 7am to 2pm).
  • SatEarly birds go fishing with a ranger, then cheer on the riders at the Forever Families Rodeo Round Up, or catch the stick horse races in Washington. Night out: dance to Texas Unlimited at La Bahia Hall (kids 12 and under free!).
  • SunChurch, then touch real animal furs at Skins & Skulls and stay at the lake for meteor shower stargazing. Just for adults: a paint and sip in Navasota.

Tiny Trailblazers toddler hike

Thu · 9 to 10am
Rocky Point/Overlook Trailhead, Lake Somerville State Park, Nails Creek Unit · Map$5 · kids 12 and under free

Fishing with a Ranger

Sat · 8:30 to 10am
Day Use Jetty by the boat ramp, Lake Somerville State Park, Birch Creek Unit · Map$5 · kids 12 and under free
Kid Approved5–89–11Outdoors

A hands-on morning for kids and beginners: simple gear, easy casting and the fish that live in Lake Somerville. Rods are provided, and bring your own bait and gear if you have them. Pack water, sunscreen and bug spray.

A rodeo with a big heart

Forever Families Rodeo Round Up

Sat · rodeo 9:30am · family fun 10am
Cowboy Church of Brenham, 4400 Hwy 105 · Map
Kid Approved0–45–89–11FundraiserOutdoors

The very first Rodeo Round Up from Here I Am Orphan Ministries: youth rodeo events, a petting zoo, hayrides, cornhole and concessions. The sweetest part is the Shining Stars, where kids with special needs or no rodeo experience ride a ministry horse with a parent or youth wrangler while the whole crowd cheers. 100% of ticket sales and sponsorships support orphans around the world and families who adopt or foster. Riders sign up online.

Details

Two meteor showers at once

Fireballs of Fall: A Stargazing Program

Sun · 7 to 8:30pm
Birch Creek Day Use Area, Lake Somerville State Park · Map$5 · kids 12 and under free
5–89–11After dark

Watch for the Leonid and Taurid meteor showers with a ranger and hear the stories behind them, plus why fall is such a good time for "fireballs." Look for the white Texas State Parks truck. Bring lawn chairs, bug spray and binoculars or a telescope if you have them.

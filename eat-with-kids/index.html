Where to Eat with Kids

Ever taken the baby to a restaurant bathroom for a diaper change and... no changing table? Same. So one of our readers, Chloe, had the best idea: a list of the most family-friendly restaurants, coffee shops and breweries around Brenham, checked by real parents.

What we're looking for

  • Diaper duty: a changing table (gold star if the men's room has one too!)
  • Baby-ready: stroller accessible, a comfy spot to nurse, somewhere to walk a fussy baby
  • Kid food: kids menu, fast service, dessert included, kids-eat-free nights
  • Room to play: a play area or somewhere to run
  • Friendly vibe: staff who are sweet to kids (and loud enough nobody hears yours)
  • Easy in, easy out: easy parking, early dinner hours

Spots that hit 4 of the 6 get the Kid Approved footprint. We only celebrate what places get right, and nobody can pay to be on it.

Well, hey there

Welcome to the very first Brenham Every Day. Just consider us the light at the end of your "what is there to do in Brenham, TX?" tunnel. We're a day late and a dollar short on this one, but there's still a whole lot of weekend left.

Look for the little footprint if you're bringing the kiddos. The every-week stuff is further down, for when you need a plan B.

Only in Brenham, folks

Zero Turn Barrel Race

Sun · Books open 3pm, race 4pm
Cowboy Church of Brenham, 4400 Hwy 105 (just past Silver Wings) · MapFree to watch · $25 to race
Kid Approved0–45–89–11Racers 18+Outdoors

Yes, barrel racing. On zero-turn mowers. The Cowboy Church of Brenham runs two classes (up to 26 hp, and 27 hp and up), $25 gets you two runs with your best one counting, and the top three in each class take home cash and a banner. Racers need a seat belt and roll bar, blades off, and no deck over 72 inches. Everybody else (kids very much included): pull up a lawn chair and watch.

Tip: Questions? The flyer says to call Johnny Prejean at (936) 994-2228.

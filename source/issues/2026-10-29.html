Hey, y'all

To our very first subscribers: hi!! Thank you for signing up before we even sent a single email. You're the best, and this one's for you.

Halloween lands on a Saturday this year, which means the whole dang weekend is costumes. Families, we've got trick-or-treat on Main Street, pancakes with the Washington fire department, and a whole roundup of trunk-or-treats, including one with horses and one you can do without unbuckling the car seat. Grown-ups, we've got a creepy little thriller at Unity Theatre, a Grease-themed Wine & Wander in Burton and one last weekend of antiques. Everybody wins.

Everything up top happens this weekend only. Look for the little footprint if you're bringing the kiddos.

Ew. It's Survival Sunday. Halloween and the end of daylight saving land back to back this year, so it's candy meltdowns Saturday night, then the clocks fall back an hour early Sunday morning. Kids never get the memo, so you'll be up at 5am anyway. Cartoons are your friend. Then nap. Everybody. For those of y'all without young kids, enjoy the extra hour. 😭

Super spooky weekend roundup

Past the usual candy lines: a Victorian funeral tour, a stagecoach and a police-run haunted house.

The spookiest history lesson around

Memento Mori: Funeral Tours + Stagecoach Rides

Fri to Sun · 10am to 4pm
Fanthorp Inn State Historic Site, Anderson · MapStagecoach $10, 5 and under free
5–89–11Tickets in advanceWorth the drive · 45 min

The 1834 Fanthorp Inn marks 159 years since Henry and Rachel Fanthorp died with tours on the peculiar ways Victorian families mourned their dead. Deliciously creepy. On Saturday only, 11 to 3, you can ride an actual stagecoach.

Tip: The tour itself may be a lot for kids under 10, so the little ones can ride the stagecoach while the grown-ups take the tour. All tickets have to be bought ahead online.

Hey, y'all

Halloween lands on a Saturday this year, which means the whole dang weekend is costumes. Families, we've got trick-or-treat on Main Street and pancakes with the Washington fire department. Grown-ups, we've got a creepy little thriller at Unity Theatre, a Grease-themed Wine & Wander in Burton and one last weekend of antiques. Everybody wins.

Everything up top happens this weekend only. Look for the little footprint if you're bringing the kiddos. The every-week stuff is further down, for when you need a plan B.

Heads-up: clocks fall back an hour early Sunday morning. Kids never get the memo, so plan for a 5:30am Sunday. mmm. coffee.

The whole town in costume

Mask-erade on Main

Sat · 2 to 5pm
Historic Downtown Brenham · MapFree
Kid Approved0–45–89–11Stroller OK

Main Street businesses hand out candy all afternoon. It's daylight, the sidewalks are flat, and it ends by 5, so toddlers get the full experience and still make bedtime. Older kids can hit the neighborhoods after.

Tip: Park a few blocks off Main and walk in. Bring a bag that's bigger than you think you need.

Breakfast before the sugar

Pancakes and Fire Trucks at Washington VFD

Sat · 10am
Station #2, 15505 Hwy 105, Washington · MapFree breakfast
Kid Approved0–45–89–11

The Washington Volunteer Fire Department serves a free pancake breakfast, with fire truck tours, kids' activities, and a water truck spraying station. They're raising money for equipment and training with a gun drawing at noon ($20 tickets). You can skip that part and just come for the pancakes.

Tip: Pack a change of clothes. The spraying station means wet kids.

Shows and stages

A date-night thriller

Mirror Lake at Unity Theatre

Thu 7:30 · Fri + Sat 8pm · Sun 2pm
Unity Theatre Studio, Brenham · Map$27, $23 Thursday preview
Grown-upsIndoors

A couple marks every anniversary by staging scares for each other, and on their tenth, at a lakeside cabin, the game goes too far. It's the right show for Halloween weekend. Thursday's preview is cheaper and has a talkback with the cast afterward.

Tip: The Studio is small, so book ahead.

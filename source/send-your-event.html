Send us your event

Hosting something fun in or around Brenham? We'd love to hear about it. Festivals, trunk or treats, fundraisers, story times, live music, the little church carnival nobody knows about yet. If it's happening within about 30 miles, send it our way.

What to send

  • The name of the event and who's hosting
  • The date, start and end time
  • Where it is (the address helps)
  • What it costs, or that it's free
  • A line or two about what's going on, especially anything for the kids
  • A flyer, a link, or your Instagram handle so we can tag you

How to send it

Message us on Instagram or Facebook, whichever you like.

Message us on Instagram Message us on Facebook

The guide goes out every Wednesday, so a week or more ahead is perfect.

Farm spotlight · Nov 12, 2026

Whitehurst Farm fresh from the farm

Not an ad! I'm just really passionate about buying local, and I know y'all are too.

Whitehurst Farm is owned and run by Michael and Leslie Marchand, right here in Brenham. What began as a way to feed their own family well has grown into a full-time farm. They farm the regenerative way, working to heal the land and not just keep it going, and they grow their greens and root crops without chemicals.

Who grows your food?

A lot of us think of the grocery store as where our vegetables come from. But real people grow them: long hours, lessons learned the hard way, and working through harsh weather, wet ground and sandy soil. Local customers are a big part of what keeps small farms like this one going.

What they raise

  • Grass-fed beef
  • Pasture-raised chicken and pork
  • Pasture-raised eggs
  • Organic vegetables and herbs

Good to know for the holidays

They didn't raise holiday turkeys this year, but they have specialty meat cuts that people love during the holidays. And they say fall going into winter is actually one of the best times for fresh produce, especially lettuce and leafy greens!

How to order

  • Order online at whitehurstfarm.com. The farm store updates every week.
  • Order by Wednesday for Saturday pickup at the farm.
  • Or order by Saturday for Wednesday pickup at the farm.
  • Home delivery in Brenham comes Tuesdays (order by Saturday, $125 minimum), and they deliver to parts of the Houston area too.
  • Everything is harvested and packed to order.

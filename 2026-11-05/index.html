Hey, y'all

Halloween is over, the candy is (mostly) gone, and somehow it's still 90 degrees. This weekend is roses, ranches and the lake. Families, the Fall Festival of Roses is our main pick, plus glowing scorpions and a fishing morning with a ranger at Lake Somerville, Play Day at Cowboy Church and hayrides at a holiday market on a ranch in Burton. And there's something new for grown-ups too: a brand new For the adults section with a wine night, a Texas dance hall, a gala and a cozy pie night before Thanksgiving.

My picks for each day

  • ThuEarly birds, take the toddlers on the Tiny Trailblazers hike at Lake Somerville. Looking for a night out? It's the first Thursday, so it's Burton Wine Society night.
  • FriDrop in for story time at Hermann, then grab bread and goodies at the First Friday market. After dark, go find scorpions that glow at the lake.
  • SatStart at the Fall Festival of Roses or watch the little cowboys and cowgirls at Play Day. End with sunset hayrides at the Ranch Gathering in Burton. Night out: Big Star Texas Night at a historic dance hall.
  • SunChurch, then Fishing with a Ranger (no license needed) or one more lap through the rose gardens, which open early at 9:30 just for the festival.

Hey, y'all

The last weekend before Thanksgiving, and it's a sweet one! A turkey treasure hunt at the lake that runs all the way through Thanksgiving week, 1840s chili at Fanthorp Inn, Christmas car ornaments at Home Depot, a glow party for the kids and a homemade butter class for the grown-ups. Friday looks sunny and in the mid 70s, and the weekend looks pleasant so far (upper 70s Saturday, mostly sunny Sunday), so check the forecast before you head out.

Still need a Thanksgiving dinner? A few pre-orders close this week (some on Friday!), so check our Thanksgiving page.

Our main pick: follow Lucky the turkey's clues

The Great Turkey Treasure Hunt

Sat Nov 21 to Sat Nov 28 · any time
Lake Somerville State Park, Birch Creek AND Nails Creek units (first clue at each HQ) · MapFree with park entry: $5 · kids 12 and under free
Kid Approved0–45–89–11Outdoors

A self-guided scavenger hunt: Lucky, a clever turkey, has hidden clues along the overlooks, bridges, shorelines and trails. Grab the first clue at HQ, follow them to the end and claim the Turkey Treasure. It runs all through Thanksgiving week, so it's a great one for when the kids are out of school. Bring water, closed-toe shoes, a park map and your phone for pictures.

Hi, y'all!

We're Brenham Every Day. We made the thing we couldn't find: one place for everything going on in and around Brenham, especially the family-friendly stuff.

Why we made this

We moved here with a toddler, a baby, and a (somewhat) desperate need for the occasional date night. Every weekend I'd end up digging through a dozen calendars, Facebook groups and flyers taped to coffee shop doors just to figure out what was going on. ESPECIALLY for the kids. I've had this idea for a long time, so I finally made the thing I kept wishing existed.

Every Wednesday

Every Wednesday you'll get the best of what's happening that weekend in and around Brenham. Think Chappell Hill, Burton, Independence, Washington, Round Top, Bellville and Navasota.

Made for families

It's made with families in mind, so look for the little footprint on our Kid Approved picks. Those are the real standouts, not just places kids are allowed, and we'll tell you which ages they're best for.

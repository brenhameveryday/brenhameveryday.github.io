Thanksgiving Buffet Lunch

Thu Nov 26 · 11am to 2pm
Hugo's 1876, The Grand Fayette Hotel, Fayetteville · Map$75, under 10 free

An annual Thanksgiving buffet lunch in the historic Grand Fayette Hotel: herb roasted turkey and honey baked ham, cornbread dressing and gravy, sweet and mashed potatoes, green beans, roasted Brussels sprouts, cherry Jello salad, cranberry sauce, yeast rolls, pumpkin and pecan pie. $75 a person, children under 10 eat free. ThuFree Community Thanksgiving MealCannery Kitchen, Brenham · Free

Free Community Thanksgiving Meal

Thanksgiving Day · time TBA, we’ll update it here
The Cannery Kitchen, 314 E Alamo St, Brenham · MapFree
Free

Faith Mission's Cannery Kitchen has served a free Thanksgiving meal to anyone who'd like one for more than 15 years, together with Gather n Grace. Come eat, or come help: volunteers prep food, serve and visit with guests, deliver meals and clean up (sign up on their page). Homebound neighbors can get a meal delivered free by calling Faith Mission at 979-277-7330.

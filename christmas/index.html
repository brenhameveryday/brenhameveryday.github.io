Christmas Lane of Lights

Fri + Sat, Nov 27 to Dec 19 · 6 to 8:30pm
FM 1291 & Hwy 290, downtown Ledbetter · Map$10, 2 and under free
Outdoors

Ledbetter Volunteer Fire Department lights up downtown Ledbetter every Friday and Saturday night (Nov 27-28, Dec 4-5, 11-12 and 18-19). Music and Santa every night. $10 a person, kids 2 and under free, cash or check. All proceeds benefit the Ledbetter Volunteer Fire Department.

6pmTwilight at Fanthorp InnAnderson · $10 wagon, $20 stagecoach

Twilight at Fanthorp Inn

Sat Dec 12 · 6 to 9pm
Board at the Grimes County Courthouse, Anderson · Map$10 wagon, $20 stagecoach
Worth the drive

Ride a replica stagecoach or wagon down Main Street to the historic Fanthorp Inn for a candlelit 1800s holiday celebration with fiddle music and living history characters. $10 wagon ride or $20 stagecoach ride (admission included); kids 5 and under ride free on a lap. Tickets are sold in time slots and must be bought ahead, not at the event. Arrive 30 minutes before your boarding time.
